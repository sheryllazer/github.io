Assumptions and sources9 drivers
Driver2025 → 2030Basis
Urban population526 → 612 mnUNFPA and World Bank urban share, applied to total population.
Metro-8 penetration25% → 34%Solved jointly with frequency against 3 bn trips and Delhi NCR's 16–18% contribution. Equals 49% of smartphone-owning adults today, converging to a ~52% ceiling.
Tier-1 penetration16% → 26%Same solve. 37% of smartphone-owning adults today.
Tier-2+ penetration7% → 15%20% of smartphone-owning adults today. Driven by the consuming class, smartphones, zero-commission supply and bike-taxi legalisation.
Metro frequency4.3 → 5.0 / monthAnchored on Rapido's disclosed 4.06 rides per transacting user (207 mn rides ÷ 17 mn users, Q2 FY25), adjusted up for its bike and auto skew. No assumption of primary-mode substitution.
Cab fare₹300–420, +4% p.a.Regulated tariffs of ₹21–32/km across Karnataka and Pune, over a 13–15 km average trip, plus the airport and rental mix that skews metros up.
Auto fare₹95–135, +5% p.a.Bengaluru meter of ₹36 base plus ₹18/km; app fares run 20–30% over meter on a 5–6 km trip.
Bike-taxi fare₹55–78, +5% p.a.Maharashtra notified tariff of ₹15 base plus ₹10.27/km over a 5–7 km trip.
Category mixBike taxi 28% → 34% of ridesCabs over-index in metros — 45% of Delhi NCR trips against 27% nationally. Bike taxi is the share gainer everywhere outside them.
