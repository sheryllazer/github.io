AI workflow · Sourcing

A sourcing sweep, run as a skill

Traditional job boards don’t work for the roles I want anymore. The best openings at funds and early-stage startups rarely get posted there, and when they do, they’re buried under hundreds of others. So I built my own.

It crawls fund career pages and startup boards on a schedule and scores every opening against five filters I set. Anything that fails is screened out in a single line, with the reason recorded, before any time goes into tailoring.
